Mark A Spicer

I am a PhD researcher at the University of Kent investigating why people fall for phishing emails. My systematic literature review — applying the PRISMA methodology across 4,402 screened articles — examines the psychological, demographic, behavioural, and environmental factors that drive susceptibility, and evaluates the effectiveness of organisational controls in reducing risk. My work calls for large-scale, real-world research to close the gaps left by studies conducted predominantly in educational settings. Alongside my research, I work as a Data Protection Officer, bringing a practical lens to questions of human behaviour and information security.
